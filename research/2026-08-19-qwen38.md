# Qwen3.8-27B, desk research 2026-08-19

Evidence label: not run on this rig. The machine was powered off that day. It was dismantled on 2026-08-27. Nothing below is a measurement of ours.

Qwen3.8-27B weights went public 2026-08-14 (`Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B`, Apache-2.0). The architecture matches Qwen3.6-27B: GGUF `general.architecture=qwen35`, 64 layers plus a next-token head, hybrid GDN and full attention, native vision. A 3.6 llama.cpp recipe is a weight swap. There is no Qwen3.8-35B-A3B, so the fast MoE slot in `profiles/qwen-35b-a3b.md` has no 3.8 successor in this research.

New knobs: `reasoning_effort` of `low`, `medium`, or `xhigh` (default xhigh), and `preserve_thinking`. `high` raises on the official template and silently becomes xhigh on Unsloth's embedded template. `medium` was the setting club-3090 and froggeric converged on. `xhigh` was reported using on the order of 10× the thinking tokens of 3.6.

Sampler pairs move in opposite directions. Thinking: temp 1.0 / top-p 0.95 / top-k 20 / presence 0. Non-thinking: temp 0.7 / top-p 0.80 / top-k 20 / presence 1.5. Do not reuse one pair for the other mode.

Official benches versus 3.6-27B (vendor table): SWE-bench Pro 61.7 vs 53.5, Terminal Bench 2.1 73.0 vs 63.4, LiveCodeBench v6 90.3 vs 83.9. An independent single-3090 HumanEval (insiderllm) was a tie, 80.49 vs 82.32, p=0.70, and about 41.5 vs 41.8 TPS at UD-Q4_K_XL with MTP off. The gain they are selling is agent recovery, and it was not checked against our actual pair (A3B plus 27B-ik).

club-3090 at HEAD `e17e0ee` that day had adopted 3.8 mostly as dual and multi-card vLLM. The only single-3090 slug was `llamacpp/qwen38-27b-single-iq4nl`, incubating, booted at 131K with q8_0 KV, MTP accept 0.69, and **no TPS published**. 196608 failed on prefill buffers. 262144 failed on the recurrent cache. On 2026-08-12 they had retired the single-card llama.cpp and ik Qwen3.6 slugs from the default catalog. The files still launch with `--force`. Our composes do not depend on that catalog.

Community single-3090 speeds gathered that day, all someone else's hardware: llama.cpp MTP n=2 roughly 55–65 TPS on a current build, higher with an overclock, ik IQ4_KS unpublished and guessed near our 61/76. That is parity with `profiles/qwen-27b-ik.md`, not a speed upgrade. syv-ai's patched vLLM stack claimed about 114 TPS and 150K at 23 GiB, with nine local patches. We did not apply them. Single-card vLLM on our rig already had the Cliff 2b history.

Flags copied from their single-card compose, for a later test, not as a recipe we ran: `-c 131072 -b 4096 -ub 512 -ngl 99 -fa on`, q8_0 KV, `--spec-type draft-mtp --spec-draft-n-max 2`, `--reasoning off`, non-thinking samplers, `LLAMA_ARG_CHAT_TEMPLATE_KWARGS={"reasoning_effort":"low"}`. They kept the native template because froggeric's template would drop `reasoning_effort`. MTP draft KV stays f16. n-max 2 on 24 GB. `draft-mtp,ngram-mod` was reported to segfault on a 3090.

The planned first run was an additive llama-swap entry, ubergarm `Qwen3.8-27B-MTP-IQ4_KS`, 131K, q8_0 KV, MTP n=2, reasoning off, `reasoning_effort=medium`, benched with the same harness as the ik session against `qwen3.6-27b-ik`. It never happened.
